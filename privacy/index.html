Antojo Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Antojo collects only what it needs to let you rank places and share them with friends. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

Who we are

Antojo is run by Antoni Borrell Tatche, Calle Bravo Murillo 60, 28003 Madrid, Spain, who is responsible for your data. Contact: ton@bonic.app.

What we collect

  • Account: your Sign in with Apple ID and, if you share it, your email.
  • Profile: name, username and an optional photo.
  • What you add: rankings, notes, saved places, photos, who you follow or block, and reports you make.
  • Location: used on your phone to centre the map. We only receive the map area on screen, and we don't store it.
  • Crash reports: app and device details when the app crashes, without your name, email or user ID.

How we use it

To run the app, suggest places from your rankings and your friends', keep people safe (reports and blocks) and fix crashes. We do this to provide the service you signed up for, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe and working.

What others see

Other Antojo users can see your name, username, photo, rankings, notes, ranking photos and who you follow. Photos sit at unlisted links that anyone with the link can open. Your email is never shown, and people you block can't see your profile.

Who we share it with

Only the providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and photos, EU), Railway (servers, EU), Sentry (crash reports, EU), Apple (sign-in and maps) and Google (place details). Anthropic receives public place details to sort places by type, never anything about you. When data leaves the EU, it is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. Delete it any time in Me > Settings > Delete account: your profile, rankings, photos and everything else linked to you are erased at once.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to ton@bonic.app. You can also complain to Spain's data protection authority, the AEPD.

Children

Antojo is not for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app about important changes.
